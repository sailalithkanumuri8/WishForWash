# Image credits

## Wish for Wash poster art

These files come from the team's Wish for Wash information poster (LMC 3403, Group 2,
Fall 2026) and are regenerated from it by `tools/extract-poster-assets.py`:

| File | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `assets/img/globe-in-hands.webp`, `globe-in-hands-640.webp` | The painted globe-in-hands, cut out with a translucent glass dome. `globe-in-hands.json` gives the sphere, water-surface ellipse, drop box and ripple centre for animated overlays. |

| `public/og-image.jpg` | Open Graph card composed from the wordmark and the globe-in-hands. |

## Georgia Tech logo

`gt-logo.svg`, `gt-logo-reversed.svg` and `gt-logo-horizontal.svg` are built from the
official vector file **Georgia Tech logo 2021.svg** on Wikimedia Commons
(<https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Georgia_Tech_logo_2021.svg>), which credits the
Georgia Tech Brand Guide (<https://brand.gatech.edu/our-look/logos>). Commons lists the file
as public domain (a text logo), but the GT marks are registered trademarks of the Georgia
Institute of Technology, so use them as Georgia Tech's brand guidelines allow: unaltered
proportions, official colors, clear space around the mark.

- `gt-logo.svg` and `gt-logo-reversed.svg`: the stacked "GT / Georgia Tech" lockup printed
  on the poster, recomposed only from the Commons file's own paths (the interlocking GT,
  "Georgia", "Tech" taken from "Technology", and the ® marks), scaled and placed to match
  the poster. Tech Gold `#B3A369` mark with Navy `#003057` lettering, or white lettering for
  dark backgrounds.
- `gt-logo-horizontal.svg`: the Commons lockup ("Georgia Institute of Technology") with its
  shapes unchanged, recolored to Tech Gold and Navy.
